Rocket Mortgage • 2024

Rocket Connect

Bid Desk enables users to efficiently manage, bid, and pair out loan portfolios from selling clients.

Role Lead UX Designer
Timeline 6 months
Team 3 designers, 8 engineers
01

Overview

The Challenge

Rocket Mortgage needed a streamlined platform for managing loan portfolio bids. The existing process was fragmented across multiple tools, leading to inefficiencies and missed opportunities.

The Solution

We designed Rocket Connect — a unified bidding platform that consolidates portfolio management, bid submission, and pair-out workflows into a single, intuitive interface.

02

Understanding the Problem

67% of users reported workflow friction
4+ different tools used daily
23min average time per bid submission
"I spend more time switching between systems than actually analyzing portfolios." — Senior Loan Officer, Discovery Interview
03

Research & Discovery

Research Methods

  • 12 stakeholder interviews across 3 departments
  • Contextual inquiry sessions with 8 power users
  • Competitive analysis of 5 industry platforms
  • Journey mapping for 3 primary user personas

Key Insights

Users needed real-time visibility into portfolio status, streamlined bid workflows, and better collaboration tools. The biggest pain point was context-switching between disparate systems.

04

Design Process

✏️

Ideation

Collaborative sketching sessions to explore multiple concepts

🔲

Wireframing

Low-fidelity prototypes to validate information architecture

🎨

Visual Design

High-fidelity mockups aligned with brand guidelines

🧪

Testing

Usability testing with 15 participants across 3 rounds

05

The Solution

Unified Dashboard

A single view that surfaces active portfolios, pending bids, and key metrics. Users can quickly assess status and take action without navigating away.

Streamlined Bidding

A guided workflow reduces bid submission from 23 minutes to under 8 minutes. Smart defaults and inline validation prevent errors.

06

Results & Impact

65% reduction in bid submission time
89% user satisfaction score
$2.4M estimated annual efficiency gains
07

Reflections

What I Learned

This project reinforced the importance of early stakeholder alignment. By involving compliance and engineering from the discovery phase, we avoided costly pivots later in the process.

What I'd Do Differently

I would have introduced more structured design critiques earlier. Some visual inconsistencies emerged late that could have been caught sooner with regular review sessions.
